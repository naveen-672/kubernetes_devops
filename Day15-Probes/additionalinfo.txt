⭐ Easy notes to remember

Readiness probe passes → Pod becomes Ready → EndpointSlice marks it ready → kube-proxy/network dataplane updates routing → Service can send traffic to the Pod.

Readiness probe fails → Pod becomes NotReady → it is not an eligible ready endpoint → networking stops sending normal Service traffic to it.

And remember:

Readiness probe does not fix the application. It only controls whether the Pod should receive traffic.
